# Pre-score development evaluation plan

Decision: illustrative replenishment at 2025-12-01 using the supplied November 30 stock and policy, for December 1–7. These inputs cannot support an October 2026 order. All operational files describe synthetic scenarios, so quantities will be explicitly illustrative.

Sources: only four released CSVs. Read every row; profile types, missingness, quantity signs, status/return counts, exact and identifier duplicates, dates, names, units, key coverage and incoming-stock consistency. Drop exact duplicates, normalize statuses; target positive non-cancelled ordered units including processing and returned orders. This measures conditional recorded orders, not unconstrained demand. No historical stockout correction or status-availability proof is possible.

Panel: all 336 supplied product/store keys. Calendar days after each key's first appearance may be assigned zero recorded orders only under the explicit assumption of a complete extract, which is not proven. No zero assignment before appearance. Quantify observed-day-only rate sensitivity as an alternative missingness scenario, not a better forecast or truth.

Candidates: local means over trailing 28, 91 (baseline), 182 and 364 calendar days; shared product/store rate and shared seasonal rate exactly as specified in the approved task. Shared = 0.5 local364 + 0.5 network91 × product364 share × store364 share. Seasonal factors use network mean daily units by month in completed calendar years before the origin year, normalized by the unweighted average of twelve monthly means; multiply shared by future factors divided by trailing91 mean factor. Every forecast uses orders strictly before its origin. Missing days in those completed calendar years require the same complete-extract assumption.

Initial selection: Mondays June 30–September 15, 2025. Later development check: Mondays October 6–November 24, 2025. Target: seven days beginning at origin. Primary score: mean absolute error, equal weight per key and origin. Select initial lowest-MAE candidate; retain baseline unless that candidate reduces MAE by at least 5% in BOTH periods. Bias, RMSE and lead-plus-review diagnostic scores cannot override the rule. Neither period is untouched validation; no December outcomes will be accessed. Save per-origin/key/candidate source-derived predictions and scores.

Ordering: forecast expected recorded units across supplier lead time plus seven-day review, add supplied safety stock, subtract usable stock and eligible scheduled receipts, floor at zero, then round up to minimum and pack multiple only for positive orders. Use receipt timing for pre-arrival shortage flags and stress checks. Do not reuse transactional inventory values as current usable stock. No cost/service optimization or demonstrated savings claim. Compare against baseline interpretation of recent months, not an invented exact customer incumbent.

Validate integer/nonnegative units, all keys, pack/minimum rules, join cardinality, source hashes and actual output SHA-256. Record measured runtime, dependencies and data size. Routine model is deterministic pandas/numpy with no language-model or paid model API dependency; separate build cost unavailable without observed AI billing usage/pricing.
