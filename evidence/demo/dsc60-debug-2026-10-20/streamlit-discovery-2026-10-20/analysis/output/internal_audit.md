# Retail restocking investigation

## Scope and source authority

The four approved released CSVs are the only business sources used. Their byte hashes and line-level cleaning counts are in `source_audit.json`. The transaction release is a controller-derived development partition; its byte hash differs from the supplied original-source hash, as disclosed in the request. No original file or December sales was sought. Future receipt due dates are operational inputs already known on November 30, not sales outcomes. The analysis is as of November 30, 2025, irrespective of execution date.

`task_refinement.json` preserves the original question and supplied customer context, separately attributes analyst interpretation, and distinguishes supplied facts, synthetic inputs and unknowns. The real decision is integer units per product at store, now, to support next week's availability while avoiding unnecessary stock. Neither a service level nor a tradeoff between stockouts and excess stock was supplied. Unit forecasting error is a useful quantity-planning proxy, not an estimate of savings, profit, service or optimal purchases.

## Preparation and target

17,406 transaction rows reduce to 17,364 after removing 42 exact repeated rows. There are no conflicting unique transaction IDs. The 3,224 repeated order IDs are retained as distinct transaction lines. Core fields have no nulls. All 28 products have one consistent nonmissing name; the mapping fills 135 missing line descriptions. No customer personal information is exported.

Normalize case and surrounding whitespace in order statuses. Exclude 851 cancelled lines and eight nonpositive quantity lines, without treating the three negative quantities as trustworthy usable returns. The positive noncancelled target contains 16,505 lines and 35,143 gross units. Include processing, shipped, completed and returned orders: these represent gross requested supply, rather than subtracting returns whose receipt date and condition are unknown. Return-flagged target units total 3,649; processing contributes 2,945 units. These ambiguities are evaluated as prespecified alternate targets, not silently collapsed into net demand.

The all-channel total assumes a store identifier is the fulfillment location for that channel. The supplied transactions cannot verify that assumption. Channel-specific stock usage would require a fulfillment mapping before operational deployment. Status transition/update times are absent. All training rows are restricted by order date at each origin, but cancellation filtering uses final supplied statuses. Thus this is a chronological development comparison on a static extract, not a proven historical real-time replay. None of the methods uses future status as a feature, stock snapshots, delivery outcomes, prices, ratings, customer fields or future receipts as forecasting features.

All 1,430 network calendar days have at least one record, but individual stores have only 638–1,080 observed days. Network continuity does not establish complete store coverage. Do not impute sales on absent store-days. Zero recorded contributions are assigned only to already-observed products during store-weeks that have records; they mean no supplied transaction contribution, not proof of zero underlying demand. Entire unobserved store-weeks are missing (14 in the 204-week panel), and the first partial observation week for each pair is omitted. Weekly/horizon targets sum only recorded units on available days. A target horizon with no store records is excluded for every method. This avoids invented demand zeros but cannot repair unknown underrecording, closures, assortment availability or lost sales. It forecasts the supplied recording process under similar coverage. Complete-store-week diagnostics and a separate missing-day scenario expose this limitation.

The current population is all 336 policy/stock pairs. All have sales observations by March 21, 2023, before comparison origins and before the 104-week model windows. This is explicitly conditional on the supplied current assortment; no historical assortment table proves that the current population was active throughout earlier periods. The descriptive sparsity grouping uses 2025 development observations and has no role in training or selection.

## Prospective comparison and bounded discovery

`prepare.py` wrote the refinement and `evaluation_plan.json` before scoring. Source inspection motivated four fixed candidates: the recent 13-week per-pair mean baseline; exponential smoothing at fixed 0.15; 26-week local means shrunk with 13-week weight toward product/store pooled means; and a regularized pooled Poisson regression with product/store effects, two annual Fourier harmonics and time trend. These represent recency, variance reduction and recurring calendar patterns. Prior November/September daily-unit ratios are approximately 1.51, 1.51 and 1.66 in 2022–24, supporting investigation of seasonality without dictating its success. No tuning grid, ensemble, neural model or iterative search was used.

The customer described recent-month review without specifying a window, aggregation, ordering rule or discretionary adjustments. The exact incumbent cannot be scored. The analytical baseline is clearly labeled and remains fixed. There is no claim to have beaten the customer's process.

Seven Sunday origins, October 5 through November 16, use information by each origin. Primary forecast horizon is the following seven days. Secondary horizon is the supplied current lead plus seven-day review, varying from 8 to 14 days. The same origins ensure that even the longest comparison ends November 30. The current synthetic policy is used as a coverage scenario, not asserted to have existed at historical origins. Partial future weeks are proportional allocations of weekly predictions; no daily pattern is inferred from missing dates. There is no unevaluated horizon extension in the order coverage, although the selected method was ranked on seven-day performance rather than a multi-objective score.

Primary selection uses pooled absolute recorded-unit error with equal weight per eligible pair-origin. Numerical ties within 1e-9 favor the baseline. Results include 2,324 seven-day cases and 2,347 coverage cases per method, with identical targets and membership within each horizon. These horizons can have different eligible counts because a store without first-week records can have second-week records. Every historical fit precedes its forecast dates. October–November was already inspected in this project and is development evidence, regardless of any local terminology.

One code-review correction replaced fixed-denominator pooled totals with observed-only pooled means to avoid interpreting an unobserved store-week as a zero contribution in the pooling calculation. `implementation_correction.json` records the repair before the corrected run; `evaluation_plan_v2.json` consolidates that record afterward, explicitly labeled as such. Specifications and development status remain unchanged. Initial scores and the initial code (reconstructed at the single documented repair block) are retained in `initial_implementation`; corrected root outputs control the deliverable. This was a missingness repair, not an adaptive model search.

## Results and interpretation

Corrected next-week mean absolute errors are: baseline 0.963640 units; exponential smoothing 0.980696; pooled shrinkage 0.942940; calendar Poisson 1.036121. The declared rule selects pooled shrinkage, a 2.148% reduction from the analytical baseline. A reduction of about 0.021 unit per case is small. No business meaningfulness threshold was supplied, and none is invented. The baseline remains a valid benchmark; the more elaborate calendar model did not help this metric.

The selected method also reduces coverage-horizon absolute error; exact values are in `comparison_summary.csv`. Both the selected model and baseline underforecast totals during the development period, and the selected method has a more negative bias. Symmetric absolute error can reward more conservative predictions on sparse data, so the error ranking does not establish better stock availability. About 94% of product contributions on observed store-days are zero recorded units; this statistic says nothing about actual stock availability.

The selected method is lower than baseline in each of the seven origins and in both calendar-month origin groups. Prespecified exclusion of processing and exclusion of returns each retain the same lowest-error method; these follow-on target sensitivities are exploratory, not new selection exercises. A paired bootstrap over seven origins is retained in `diagnostics.json`; dependence, limited origins and selection on those same outcomes prevent interpreting it as independent success evidence. On the complete-store-week subset (only nine store-weeks, 252 pair cases), the selected model's advantage falls below half a percent. That materially limits confidence that the modest improvement persists with complete coverage. The selected method has lower error in both descriptive sparsity strata, but forecasts remain noisy in units, and pooled absolute errors exceed total actual units when summed across all sparse cases.

## Conversion to illustrative purchases

Stock and policy each have unique keys for all 336 pairs. All 126 incoming lines have unique identifiers, known orders dated by planning day and due dates December 1–7. Ledger quantities exactly match stock on-order quantities, with no unmatched pair or unit discrepancy. The supplied ledger-complete indicator is true throughout. These consistency checks do not make synthetic inputs real stock. Repeated transaction inventory values are not treated as a usable stock snapshot or historical stockout log.

For each pair, calculate forecast units over lead plus review, add supplied safety stock, subtract usable on-hand stock and receipts due by coverage end. Clip net need at zero; a positive need is raised to minimum quantity and rounded up to the order multiple. No extra buffer, lost-sale cost, economic cost or stock target is invented. Unrounded model values determine purchases; displayed forecasts are rounded only afterward. This is a conventional translation through the supplied rules, not a policy optimization or operational redesign.

The list orders 880 units for 112 products at stores, with 224 zero orders. The current forecast totals about 199 recorded units next week; purchases also replenish safety buffers and meet pack/minimum constraints, so these totals need not be similar. Forecast-based daily stock paths before new order arrival flag 52 cases for attention. Same-day receipts are assumed available before that day's demand, and new orders are available on the lead-time arrival day; only earlier days contribute to the flag. It is a deterministic warning, not a stockout probability or an expedited-order recommendation.

Applying baseline forecasts to the identical stock and policy yields 873 units; quantities differ on 15 rows. A prospectively recorded source-coverage scenario assumes unknown store-days could have the same product rate as observed days; it produces 896 units and changes 15 rows. This is not imputation of historical demand, an estimate of lost sales, or a confidence interval: transaction days may be systematically busy. The scenario confirms that quantities can change with coverage assumptions even when pack sizes limit changes. No model is reselected. These tests do not justify unconditional zero purchases when the real store has recently been short.

## Local operation, maintenance and cost

`restock_model.py` independently implements the selected formula with only Python, pandas and NumPy; it reproduces every delivered order and weekly forecast. It reads 17,406 narrow-column sales rows and three small operational files, retaining a 26-by-336 training array (69,888 bytes) and 336 means (2,688 bytes). It has no network, paid API, LLM, GPU, external training service or routine scikit-learn dependency. The discovery script additionally uses scikit-learn for Poisson and psutil for measurement. The frozen selected forecast is stored in `selected_model.npz`; routine updates should refit from fresh approved data and actual stock, checking dates, units, schema, complete open orders and coverage before use.

`routine_workload.json` records preparation, fitting, inference/order calculation, imports and process-wide peak memory. The actual selected routine took about 0.43 seconds including imports and peaked at about 138 MB. Fitting was about 0.001 seconds and inference plus all order calculations about 0.124 seconds on this host. The total process high-water is a conservative upper bound for both phases, including input preparation and retained arrays; submillisecond phase timing is noisy. Full discovery with all three targets and rolling comparisons took about 10.5 seconds and peaked below 360 MB. These are local calculation times, not the duration of this AI-assisted investigation or model building.

The host has 128 GiB RAM; no 8 GB cap was enforced. Nevertheless, the measured full process and retained array sizes are far below an ordinary 8 GB CPU computer's memory budget, leaving room for the operating system. Speed will differ, and workloads hundreds of times larger require a new capacity assessment rather than linear-runtime promises. There is no evidence of a routine cloud or paid model dependency. Costs for an existing computer, electricity or hosting remain; applicable rates are unavailable, so no dollar charge, savings or zero-cost claim is made. AI-assisted analysis/build cost is separate and cannot be estimated without observed usage and applicable pricing.

## Validation and independent success gate

`validate.py` independently recomputes full-precision order arithmetic for all 336 rows, verifies pack/minimum conditions and CSV types, recomputes metrics and comparison membership, checks dates and computes the actual SHA-256. `result.json` has exactly the required three keys. `analysis.ipynb` is an executed, table-based internal companion; all customer explanation resides in `response.md`, and no narrative HTML is produced. The app can render `restocking.csv` as the same table without additional narrative content.

This investigation cannot satisfy performance-success recording readiness: the meaningful improvement threshold must be externally declared, independent outcomes must be genuinely untouched, and the required frozen artifacts must precede independent evaluation. No such threshold is supplied, development outcomes have already been inspected, and December remains withheld. Frozen code/model/plan/source hashes support reproducibility but do not close that separate gate.
